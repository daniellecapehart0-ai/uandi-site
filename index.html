What's inside

Everything about the two of you, kept in one place.

Home

Your live timer down to the second, the next anniversary, and a "Thinking of you" tap that lets them know.

Calendar

Date nights, visits and anniversaries on one shared calendar, with countdowns to what's next.

Memories

A photo-first scrapbook with albums, a timeline, your firsts, letters, love notes and inside jokes. Search every memory and caption.

Connect

Date ideas, a bucket list, places to go, gift ideas and shared lists that either of you can add to.

Games

Quick couple games like "this or that" and question cards. Nobody keeps score. You just learn something new.

Keepsakes

Every letter, love note and voice note you send each other, kept in envelopes you open together. For Founding Members.

Our promises

Built to bring you closer, not to keep score.

No relationship scores

Nobody grades your relationship. There's no compatibility number to worry about.

No streaks

Miss a day? Nothing breaks. Use it when it feels good.

No response-time tracking

u&i never shows how fast someone replied. Your time is your own.

Private to the two of you

Your shared space is locked to your two accounts. Nobody else can see in.

Getting started

Get the app

Put u&i on your home screen.

u&i isn't in the app stores yet, but you can still install it in about 30 seconds. It opens full screen with its own icon, just like any other app. No download or app store needed.

iPhone Safari

  1. Open us-app-chi.vercel.app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The u&i heart now sits on your home screen.

Android Chrome

  1. Open us-app-chi.vercel.app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Tap Install. u&i shows up with your other apps.

Both of you install it on your own phones, then sign in with your own accounts.

Questions

Good to know.

Is u&i free?

Yes. The timer, the full calendar, Memories with up to 50 photos, written love notes, date ideas, the bucket list, gift ideas, lists, check-ins, conversation cards and date night roulette are all free. A Founding Membership is optional and unlocks Keepsakes, unlimited photos, voice notes, letters, time capsules, the date board, places and every other game.

What is a Founding Membership?

It's the paid membership for our first couples. You get every extra while u&i is in beta, and you keep your founding price for as long as you stay a member, even when prices go up later. One membership covers both of you.

Can we cancel anytime?

Yes. If you cancel, you keep everything you've saved and go back to the free features.

Who can see what we add?

Only the two of you. Your shared space is tied to your two accounts, and there are no public profiles or feeds.

Who can use u&i?

Anyone 13 or older. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian needs to agree to the Terms with you.

Do we both need to sign up?

Yes. Each of you has your own account, and you connect them with a private invite code.

Is it on the App Store?

Not yet. You can still install it on your home screen in about 30 seconds, and it opens full screen like any other app. App store versions are on the roadmap.

Does it work for long-distance couples?

Yes. The shared calendar, countdowns and check-ins are built with long-distance couples in mind.

Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026

u&i is a private app for couples, run by Danielle Capehart ("we," "us"). This policy explains what information u&i collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email dcap821@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, your password (stored in scrambled, hashed form that we can't read) and the name you show in the app.
  • Your date of birth: used only to check that you're 13 or older and that adults and people under 18 are never paired. It's kept in a private place that your partner can't see.
  • Your shared space: the date your relationship started and which account you're paired with.
  • What you add: photos, memories, captions, letters, love notes, calendar events, date ideas, lists and anything else you save in the app.
  • Basic technical data: our hosting providers automatically log things like your device type, browser and IP address to keep the service running and secure.
  • Payment details: when memberships launch, payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Waitlist sign-ups: if you join the waitlist, we receive the email and answers you submit through the sign-up form.

How we use it

  • To run u&i: sign you in, connect you with your partner and show both of you what you save.
  • To answer your support requests and send account emails, like password resets.
  • To keep u&i safe and secure, and to enforce our Terms.
  • To understand, in general terms, how the app is used so we can improve it.

We do not sell your information, we do not show ads, and we do not share your information with advertisers.

Who can see your content

Everything you add to your shared space is visible to you and the partner you paired with. Nothing is public. We access stored content only when we need to, for example to fix a problem you reported, to protect someone's safety, or when the law requires it.

Services we use

u&i relies on a few trusted companies to work: Supabase (accounts, database and photo storage), Vercel (hosting), an email delivery service for account emails, Stripe (payments, once memberships launch) and a form provider for the waitlist. They process data only to provide their service to u&i.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

  • We keep your information while your account is open.
  • You can delete your account at any time from More → Delete account. Your account, your date of birth and your private items (like gift ideas and check-ins) are deleted right away.
  • If your partner is still in your shared space, the shared content you made together, like memories, photos and plans, stays with them, with your name removed. If you're the only one left, the whole space and all of its photos are deleted.
  • Copies in backups are removed within 30 days. We recommend exporting anything you want to keep before you delete.

Your choices and rights

You can view, edit, export or delete your information in the app, or email us and we'll help within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have additional legal rights over your data. We honor these requests for every user, wherever they live.

Age requirement

u&i is only for people 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If we learn that someone under 13 has an account, we will delete it. If you believe a child is using u&i, email us.

Security

Your data is encrypted in transit, your photos are kept in private storage, and each shared space is locked to its two members. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password that you don't use anywhere else.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll update the date at the top and let you know in the app or by email.
